# 🧠 Rayyan AI for Screening Articles

Rayyan is an AI-enhanced web platform specifically designed to streamline the **screening phase** of systematic reviews. Whether you're working solo or as part of a team, Rayyan saves time and improves transparency and reproducibility.

## 🚀 Key Features of Rayyan AI

| Feature                         | Description |
|----------------------------------|-------------|
| **Blind Screening Mode**         | Prevents bias by hiding other reviewers’ decisions until screening is complete. |
| **Conflict Resolution Panel**    | Highlights conflicting inclusion/exclusion decisions and offers tools for resolution. |
| **AI-Powered Ranking**           | Learns from your screening decisions to prioritize articles likely to be included. |
| **Custom Tags & Filtering**      | Tag articles (e.g., "Wrong intervention", "Review paper") and filter easily. |
| **Notes Field per Article**      | Add justifications or reviewer comments for every decision. |
| **Bulk Actions**                 | Include, exclude, or tag multiple articles at once. |
| **Mobile & Tablet Friendly**     | Fully usable on mobile browsers for on-the-go screening. |
| **Team Collaboration Tools**     | Invite collaborators, assign roles, and track screening progress. |
| **Search and Highlight Keywords**| Use search bar to locate specific terms or highlight your criteria. |
| **Deduplication Engine**         | Automatically detects and helps you merge or delete duplicate references. |

## 🔧 Before You Start

You’ll need:
- A list of search results from databases like PubMed, Scopus, or Web of Science.
- Exported in `.ris`, `.bib`, or `.csv` format.
- Defined inclusion/exclusion criteria.
- A free Rayyan account at [https://www.rayyan.ai](https://www.rayyan.ai)

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Guide to Using Rayyan AI

### 🟢 Step 1: Create an Account
1. Go to [https://www.rayyan.ai](https://www.rayyan.ai)
2. Click **Sign up** at the top right.
3. Register using your email, Google, or ORCID.
4. Confirm your email to activate the account.

---

### 🟢 Step 2: Create a New Review
1. Log in to your account.
2. Click **+ New Review** from the dashboard.
3. Enter:
   - Title of your systematic review
   - Description (optional)
   - Choose: **Public** or **Private** (recommended)
4. Click **Create Review**

---

### 🟢 Step 3: Import References
1. Inside the review project, click **Import** (top menu).
2. Upload your `.ris`, `.csv`, or `.bib` file exported from a research database.
3. Rayyan will automatically parse your file and list the references.

📝 *Tip: If importing multiple files, Rayyan will automatically detect duplicates.*

---

### 🟢 Step 4: Start Screening
1. Click on the first article in the list.
2. Read the **Title** and **Abstract**.
3. Click one of the following:
   - ✅ **Include**
   - ❌ **Exclude**
   - ❓ **Maybe**
4. Use the **Tags** option to specify reasons for inclusion/exclusion (e.g., "Wrong population", "Not peer-reviewed").
5. Add **Notes** if you want to justify your decision.
6. Repeat for each article.

---

### 🟢 Step 5: Use Filters and AI Suggestions
After screening ~50 articles:
1. Click **AI Suggestions**.
2. Rayyan will re-rank articles based on your decisions.
3. Sort by **Relevance** to prioritize likely inclusions.
4. You can also use filters like:
   - Decision status
   - Keywords
   - Tags
   - Reviewers’ decisions

---

### 🟢 Step 6: Collaboration (Optional)
1. Click **Team** > **Invite Reviewers**.
2. Add email addresses.
3. Assign roles: **Admin**, **Reviewer**, or **Viewer**.
4. Enable **Blind Screening** if needed (decisions hidden until resolved).
5. Use the **Conflicts** tab to resolve disagreements.

---

### 🟢 Step 7: Export Results
1. When screening is complete, click **Export**.
2. Choose:
   - Included records only
   - All records with decisions and tags
3. Select format: `.csv`, `.xls`, `.ris`
4. Download and save for use in full-text review or PRISMA flowchart.
