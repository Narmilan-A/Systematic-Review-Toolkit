# 📘 SciSpace: Extracting Information from Scientific Articles

**SciSpace** (formerly Typeset.io) is an AI tool that helps you **interact with PDFs** using plain language questions. It's designed to **speed up the evidence extraction** process in literature reviews and systematic syntheses.

---

## ✨ Key Features of SciSpace AI

| Feature                         | Description |
|----------------------------------|-------------|
| **Ask PDF (AI Assistant)**       | Ask contextual questions about a paper using natural language (e.g., “What methods were used?”). |
| **AI-Powered Summary Generator** | Get concise summaries of abstracts, methods, and results. |
| **Contextual Highlighting**      | Automatically highlights relevant sections of text for your query. |
| **Method Extraction**            | Extracts detailed methodology from the text for meta-analysis. |
| **Export Citations**             | Quickly copy or export reference metadata in BibTeX format. |
| **Section Navigator**           | Quickly jump to abstract, introduction, methods, or conclusion. |
| **Chat History per Paper**       | Keep a history of questions and answers asked for each PDF. |
| **Paper Similarity Recommender** | Suggests related papers based on your document content. |

---

## 🧑‍🏫 Beginner-Friendly Workflow

### Step 1: Sign Up
- Go to [https://typeset.io](https://typeset.io)
- Create a free account with email or Google

### Step 2: Upload a PDF
- Click "Ask PDF"
- Upload a research article in PDF format
- Wait for parsing (~30 seconds)

### Step 3: Ask Questions Like:
- "What population was studied?"
- "What methods were used?"
- "What’s the sample size?"
- "What was the outcome measure?"

### Step 4: Extract & Document
- Highlight the relevant answers
- Copy-paste into Excel, Google Sheets, or Zotero notes
- Add labels: `Method`, `Results`, `Study Site`, `Statistical Test`, etc.

## 🧠 Advanced Use Cases

| Use Case                      | Feature Used            |
|------------------------------|-------------------------|

| Compare multiple papers      | Upload and analyze one-by-one |
| Find funding sources         | Ask: “Who funded this research?” |
| Extract sample demographics  | Ask: “What are the participant demographics?” |
