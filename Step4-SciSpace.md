# 📘 SciSpace: Extracting Information from Scientific Articles

**SciSpace** is an AI tool that helps you **interact with PDFs** using plain language questions. It's designed to **speed up the evidence extraction** process in literature reviews and systematic syntheses.

---

## ✨ Key Features of SciSpace AI

| Feature                         | Description |
|----------------------------------|-------------|
| **Ask PDF (AI Assistant)**       | Ask contextual questions about a paper using natural language (e.g., “What methods were used?”). |
| **AI-Powered Summary Generator** | Get concise summaries of abstracts, methods, and results. |
| **Contextual Highlighting**      | Automatically highlights relevant sections of text for your query. |
| **Method Extraction**            | Extracts detailed methodology from the text for meta-analysis. |
| **Export Citations**             | Quickly copy or export reference metadata in BibTeX format. |
| **Section Navigator**           | Quickly jump to abstract, introduction, methods, or conclusion. |
| **Chat History per Paper**       | Keep a history of questions and answers asked for each PDF. |
| **Paper Similarity Recommender** | Suggests related papers based on your document content. |

---

## 🔧 Before You Start

You’ll need:
- PDF files of research papers (open access or legally downloaded)
- A free account at [https://typeset.io](https://typeset.io)

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Guide to Using SciSpace Ask PDF

### 🟢 Step 1: Sign Up and Log In
1. Go to [https://typeset.io](https://typeset.io)
2. Click **Sign Up**.
3. Create an account using Google, ORCID, or email.
4. Log in and go to the **Ask PDF** page.

---

### 🟢 Step 2: Upload a PDF
1. Click **Upload PDF**
2. Drag-and-drop or select a PDF from your computer.
3. Wait for SciSpace to process it (usually under 1 minute).
4. Once loaded, you’ll see the document viewer and chat input on the same screen.

---

### 🟢 Step 3: Ask Your Questions
Use natural language to ask:
- “What is the sample size?”
- “What methodology did the authors use?”
- “What are the main findings?”
- “Was this study randomised?”
- “What population was studied?”

The AI will:
- Highlight the answer in the document
- Show the relevant paragraph
- Provide a short summary or answer

---

### 🟢 Step 4: Review and Extract Answers
1. Read the highlighted answer in context.
2. Copy important parts into a spreadsheet or database.
3. You can also manually highlight and save important sentences.

Example table format:

| Article | Sample Size | Methodology | Results | Outcome |
|---------|-------------|-------------|---------|---------|
| Smith et al. | 200 | RCT | Reduced blood pressure | Effective |

---

### 🟢 Step 5: Repeat for Other PDFs
1. Go back to the dashboard.
2. Upload your next PDF.
3. Continue asking and extracting information from each article.
